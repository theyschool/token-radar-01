Launch Radar v3 · priced from the tokens that already exist

Which protocols are worth farming before they launch a token?

Nobody knows which of these protocols will ever issue a token, or when. What we can know is what a token would be worth if it traded like the ones that already do — and what it costs to be there early if it does. This board prices 178 pre-token protocols off the 92 launched tokens in the same snapshot, then tells you, row by row, whether farming one is worth your money and your evenings.

Verdict in plain words: FARM means the expected value clears $2M and the plan costs $400 or less. MARGINAL means the expected value clears $500K but something is thin — the odds, the size, or both. SKIP means the numbers do not pay for your time. Expected value is just implied value times launch odds: what the token would be worth, weighted by how often protocols like this one ever launch.

Every term, kept or dropped on the blind tests

Terms in the modelWalk-forwardLeave-one-outCall

Growth, age, and chain count all sounded useful. None of them moved the blind miss, and age made the walk-forward test much worse, so they are out. TVL does the work; the category nudges it. The curve is log market cap on log TVL with a small per-category offset, fitted on 92 launched tokens. Sixteen recorded tokens could not be used as comparables — fifteen with a market cap of zero and one at minus one dollar — because a price of nothing is not a price.

What the model cannot see. One launched token trades at 400 times its TVL; another at 0.3% of it. TVL does not explain those. If a protocol's value lives somewhere TVL never goes — a brand, a founder, a license — this board will miss it, in either direction.

    How the board was made, and what was left out

    What is ranked. All 40 names on the house watchlist, plus every pre-token protocol below the $50M floor that passes the same smell test the watchlist uses — no exchanges, no bridges, no liquid staking, no wrapped or staked products, no versioned copies. That is 178 rows.

    What was set aside, and why. 436 protocols did not make the board: 144 already have a token and are priced by the market instead, and the rest are exchanges, bridges, wrapped products, versioned copies, or above-floor names the house list leaves out — the snapshot carries no parent-protocol field, so above the floor we trust the house list rather than guessing. Nothing was dropped for being small or boring.

    What a price means. The implied value is what the token would be worth if it traded the way launched tokens of the same size and kind trade. The range around it is the honest part: about twelvefold either way. Read the range, not the point.

    What farming costs. Every plan lists gas per step — about $18 a step on Ethereum, about $1 on a cheap L2 — and the hours it takes. Capital is sized to the protocol: $250 for the small ones, up to $2,000 for the big ones. If the plan costs more than the expected value, the verdict says so.
